## Question

The spec positions a budgeted forecast against what a forecast buyer buys today. [How Seer and TRUEO serve a funded decision](12-how-seer-and-trueo-serve-a-funded-decision.md) covered the market incumbents. It did not cover the panel incumbents.

From primary sources (grant pages, service pages, published reports), describe:

- Metaculus Pro Forecasters and Metaculus tournaments or series;
- FRI panels, such as LEAP.

For each, report:

- the price per question, per series, or per engagement, where it is published;
- how long it takes from the question to the first forecast;
- what the buyer receives (a probability, a rationale, an update cadence);
- how the question is resolved;
- whether the buyer can choose the question and its deadline.

Report the axes the spec claims as its wedge: a worst-case cost known before publish, and a stated depth. Run it on Sonnet at medium effort.
