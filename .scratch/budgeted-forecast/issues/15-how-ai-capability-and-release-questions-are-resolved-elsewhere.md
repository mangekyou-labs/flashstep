## Question

How do existing venues resolve AI capability questions (benchmark thresholds) and release-timing questions, and where have those resolutions been disputed? Use primary sources: question pages, resolution criteria, and dispute or annulment records.

Cover at least these venues:

- **Metaculus:** its AI series and FutureEval, and how it annuls questions.
- **Polymarket:** its AI markets resolved through UMA, and any disputes about what counted as a "release" or a score.
- **Kalshi:** its AI markets.

For each venue, report:

- how often a question was disputed, annulled, or resolved against its plain reading;
- what caused it (a benchmark version change, a staged rollout, an announcement versus availability, a leaderboard edit);
- the oracle or committee that decided, and how long that took.

This feeds [Which oracle AI capability and release questions need](16-which-oracle-ai-capability-and-release-questions-need.md). Run it on Sonnet at medium effort.
