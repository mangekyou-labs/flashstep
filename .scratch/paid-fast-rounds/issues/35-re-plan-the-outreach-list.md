## Question

[The 30 qualified payers](34-the-30-qualified-payers.md) found 25 qualifying token teams, no confirmed launchpad, no Vietnamese token team, and 6 Vietnamese trading groups that are not payers. [The outreach plan](32-the-outreach-plan.md) assumed ~18 token teams, ~12 launchpads, Vietnamese and Asian first, at least 20 sends. Which parts of that plan change? Settle: (1) whether the launchpad bar drops to 3 tokens or launchpads are dropped; (2) whether "Vietnamese first" is replaced by English-first token teams; (3) whether the Vietnamese trading groups become a co-sell route or a payer class, or stay a fallback; (4) the volume re-check before first send, given the one-day snapshot; (5) whether the 25 teams (9 reachable by Telegram) still support at least 20 sends.

## Resolution

Grilled with the user on 2026-10-26. All seven recommendations were accepted. This amends [The outreach plan](32-the-outreach-plan.md) where it conflicts.

- **Launchpads:** dropped as a payer class; the bar stays at 5 qualifying tokens. Virtuals is one named target, pitched through its own pool and 3 agent tokens.
- **Language:** English-first to the token teams, AXIS Robotics first among them. Vietnamese drafts are kept for the fallback list only.
- **Trading groups:** fallback only, contacted if the first 20 sends get no reply in 2 weeks. A deal with one counts as a paid commitment only if a qualifying token team is behind it.
- **Re-check:** liquidity and 24h volume are re-pulled from DexScreener within 48 hours before each send. Targets below the bar are skipped and logged; the re-check date goes in the outreach log.
- **Cadence:** 3 waves (8, 8, the rest), at least 20 sends. The 9 Telegram-reachable teams plus Virtuals go first. If fewer than 20 stay sendable, top up from the next page of the GeckoTerminal ranking.
- **Fallback list:** stays at 6 verified Vietnamese groups; search for more only if the fallback is triggered.
- **Contacts:** the agent finds each founder or BD lead from first-party sources one wave ahead of its send. If no source names anyone, DM the project account and log "no named contact".
