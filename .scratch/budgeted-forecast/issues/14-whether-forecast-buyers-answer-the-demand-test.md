## Question

Do forecast buyers say they would pay for a budgeted forecast on an AI capability or release-timing question? This is HITL. The human sends the messages, and the agent records the result.

Checklist:

1. Fill in `[Name]` and `[link]` in the five drafts in [the outreach asset](../../buyer-and-wedge/assets/05-outreach.md) and send them: Coefficient Giving, FRI, Anthropic Economic Futures, OpenAI Economic Research, and the Metaculus partnerships form.
2. Send a sixth message to Renaissance Philanthropy, adapting the Coefficient Giving draft to an AI economic-impact example (they co-fund the Metaculus Labor Automation Hub).
3. Send one follow-up after one week. Close the window two weeks after sending.
4. Fill in the tracking table and copy it into the Answer.

- **Pass:** any "pay" with an amount, any "maybe", or Metaculus agrees to talk as a channel. The spec then becomes ready-for-agent, and the other open tickets on this map unblock.
- **Fail:** everyone says no or doesn't reply. Record "no buyer", stop the venture track, and shelve the spec.

The test was set in [One buyer and one wedge](../../buyer-and-wedge/issues/06-one-buyer-and-one-wedge.md).
