Type: grilling

## Question

Who exactly gets contacted, with what message, and what paid commitment is asked for? Settle the target list (at least 20 names), the pitch, the ask (pilot fee, deposit or signed LOI, and its minimum size), the start date of the 4-week clock, and how replies are logged. Start from [What payers say when asked](../../buyer-and-wedge/issues/05-what-payers-say-when-asked.md). Target both token teams and Base launchpads, and only payers with Base pools deep enough for a useful Stake cap, per [The product and the payer](30-the-product-and-the-payer.md).

## Assets

- [Outreach pitch drafts](../assets/32-outreach-pitch.md): first message, follow-up and standard answers, in English and Vietnamese.

## Resolution

Grilled with the user on 2026-10-26. All nine recommendations were accepted.

- **The ask:** the US$300 7-day pilot, paid upfront in USDC or ETH. The floor is a **US$100 non-refundable deposit** toward it. A signed LOI counts only if it names a price of at least US$300 and a start date. Free pilots never count. (Glossary: Paid commitment.)
- **Who qualifies:** a payer with at least one Base pool of **≥ US$100k liquidity and ≥ US$50k daily volume**. A launchpad needs at least 5 tokens that meet the bar. Payers below it are off the list, even if warm.
- **The list:** 30 qualified names, about 18 token teams and 12 Base launchpads or communities, with Vietnamese and Asian projects first. The user sends at least 20. Streamers and trading-group admins are a fallback list, used only if the first 20 sends get no reply in 2 weeks. The names come from [The 30 qualified payers](34-the-30-qualified-payers.md).
- **Channel:** the founder or BD lead. Telegram DM first, then X DM, then the project's partnership channel. Vietnamese for VN projects, English for everyone else. No cold email.
- **The pitch:** engagement and volume. The message says: Rounds on your token, a branded page, you own the contract, US$300 for 7 days. It links the demo, asks for a 15-minute call, and stays under 80 words. Drafts are in the asset.
- **Cadence:** 3 waves: 8 in week 1, 8 in week 2 with the message revised based on week-1 replies, the rest in week 3. Each thread gets one follow-up after 4 days. One price step down to US$200 is allowed, never below the floor.
- **Clock:** first send targeted for 2026-11-09, with a hard limit of 2026-11-16 if the demo slips. The kill date is 4 weeks after the actual first send.
- **Log:** `.scratch/run-the-outreach/outreach-log.md`, one row per contact: name, project, qualifying pool, channel, language, wave, sent date, follow-up date, reply gist, objection, stated amount, status (none / replied / call / committed / no). The user reports replies; the agent updates the log.
- **Legal framing:** the payer deploys and operates the contract and the developer is a software vendor. Legal questions get "you operate, check your own jurisdiction", never advice. The user's accepted Vietnam risk is not mentioned in outreach.
