# Vietnam law for points and real-money group markets

Label: wayfinder:research
Type: research
Status: closed

## Question

Under Vietnamese law, what separates a legal points-based group prediction game from illegal gambling, and is any real-money version possible?

Cover:

- Gambling and betting: Penal Code Art. 321 (gambling) and 322 (organising gambling) thresholds, Decree 06/2017 on betting, and any 2025–2026 amendments. Who is liable: the players, the group admin, the app operator?
- Points: does a points game with no cash-out stay legal? What about points bought with money, or prizes for the winners?
- Crypto: the status of crypto assets under the Law on Digital Technology Industry (2025), Resolution 05/2025 on the pilot crypto market, and whether Vietnamese users may use stablecoins or testnet tokens in an app.
- Precedent: any enforcement against social betting apps, Zalo or Facebook betting groups, or prediction-market users in Vietnam.

Summary level, with primary sources (thuvienphapluat.vn, government portals) where possible. This is not legal advice. Flag anything that needs a lawyer.

## Resolution

A points-only game with no cash-out, no bought points and no prizes looks outside Penal Code Art. 321/322 (agent's inference); real money is very likely illegal today and organiser thresholds (10+ people, 5m VND) are easy to hit in a Zalo group. Zalo betting groups are actively prosecuted. Mostly secondary sources; needs a Vietnamese lawyer before any real-money stage.
