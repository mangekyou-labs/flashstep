# How a group market flows in a Zalo chat

Label: wayfinder:prototype
Type: prototype
Status: closed
Blocked by: 01, 05, 06

## Question

What does the whole loop look like inside a Zalo group: create a question, share it into the chat, stake, see the chance move, propose the result, dispute, and settle? Which steps happen in the chat itself and which in the mini app? The loop must fit inside a few minutes of a live event, so test placing a stake without leaving the chat, the way Pumpcade takes `predict 10 yes` from stream chat. Make a rough clickable or screen-by-screen prototype the human can show to a group admin.
