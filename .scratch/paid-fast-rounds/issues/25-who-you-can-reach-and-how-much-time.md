# Who you can reach and how much time

Label: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: closed
Blocked by: none
Assignee: MrSufferer

## Question

Who can the user actually get on a call within 4 weeks, and how many hours a week go to outreach versus the demo?

Cover: named people or channels at crypto casinos, sportsbooks and prediction platforms; Vietnamese and Asian crypto communities; BuidlGuidl and Speedrun; Farcaster; Telegram trading groups. If the honest answer is "nobody in iGaming", say which payer the reach does support, because [The product and the payer](30-the-product-and-the-payer.md) picks from it.

Budget is settled: $0 cash, solo, part-time, with about half the hours on outreach until a payer commits.

## Resolution

Grilled with the user on 2026-10-26.

- **Time:** about 10 hours a week for the 4 weeks: about 5 on outreach (finding names, messages, calls) and 5 on the demo.
- **Operators:** nobody. Operators (crypto casinos, sportsbooks, prediction platforms), aggregators and studios are reachable only cold: Stake Engine's public signup, LinkedIn or Telegram messages to BD staff, iGaming Telegram groups.
- **Crypto projects (Vietnam and Asia):** the user is a member of some Vietnamese and Asian crypto communities but named no group or person. Treat this as cold reach through those communities until names are given. It is still the closest route to a payer: a crypto project paying for sponsor-funded prize Rounds as marketing.
- **BuidlGuidl, Speedrun, Base, Farcaster:** light standing. Reach to builders and grant givers only, not payers. Use it for feedback and a possible Base Builder Grant.
- **Streamers and trading-group admins:** no direct contacts. Reachable cold; second-best non-operator payer.
- **Commitment:** the user sends 20 or more messages over the 4 weeks, with the agent drafting and logging. The 4-week clock starts on the first send. (Last time, in [What payers say when asked](../../buyer-and-wedge/issues/05-what-payers-say-when-asked.md), outreach was skipped.)
- **Language:** Vietnamese for local crypto projects and communities, English for everyone else.

**Payers ranked by reach**, handed to [The product and the payer](30-the-product-and-the-payer.md), which decides whether to drop the default operator payer: (1) crypto projects, (2) streamers and trading-group admins, (3) operators, cold only and the riskiest for the user legally per [Legal exposure as a game supplier from Vietnam](29-legal-exposure-as-a-game-supplier-from-vietnam.md).
