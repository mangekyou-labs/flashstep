## Question

[Who is paid the resolution cost](04-who-is-paid-the-resolution-cost.md) chose one named resolver, with results final and no dispute window. [What this forecast competes on](13-what-this-forecast-competes-on.md) treated that as a stated weakness. AI capability and release-timing questions are fuzzier than grant milestones: benchmarks change versions, and labs roll models out in stages. Is one named resolver still enough?

The choices:

- keep one named resolver;
- a named committee or multisig;
- a bonded optimistic oracle with a dispute window, such as UMA.

Decide how each choice changes:

- the lifecycle table: whether Awaiting result gains a dispute period;
- who receives the resolution cost, and whether it becomes a bond;
- the "results are final" rule;
- the reader copy in the result box.

Also decide whether the pin guidance (exact benchmark version; what counts as a release) removes enough ambiguity to keep the single resolver.

Before deciding, apply https://ethskills.com/SKILL.md. Do not POST to its feedback endpoint without asking the human.
