# Window and stake design for minute-scale markets

Label: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: closed
Blocked by: 10, 11

## Question

Given what Telegram and the feeds allow, how do the window and stake rules work for markets that close in minutes? Start from the settled [Stake model for the first test](../../group-markets/issues/05-stake-model-for-the-first-test.md) (pari-mutuel, no fee, hard Cutoff) and decide: window lengths, whether time-weighted shares (Pumpcade's 1.0x decaying to 0.2x) are needed, how many outcomes, what happens with one side empty, the Cutoff before the window starts or ends, and the outage and tie rules.
