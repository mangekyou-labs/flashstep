# Free-to-play onchain stakes without gambling

Label: wayfinder:research
Type: research
Status: closed

## Question

How can players stake onchain on Base mainnet with no cash value, so the activity counts as real usage but isn't gambling?

Cover:

- Patterns: non-transferable or soulbound stake tokens, daily free mints, sponsored gas through a paymaster (Coinbase Developer Platform, Pimlico, others) and its cost per transaction on Base.
- Wallet onboarding from Telegram: embedded or smart wallets (Privy, Coinbase Smart Wallet, Thirdweb), steps per new player, and cost.
- Legal: whether worthless onchain stakes stay outside gambling under the reading in [Vietnam law for points and real-money group markets](../../group-markets/issues/02-vietnam-law-for-points-and-real-money-group-markets.md), and what would cross the line (transferability, a market price, prizes).
- Existing free-to-play onchain prediction or betting games and how they were treated by grant programs, if known.

## Resolution

Superseded on 2026-10-24 before any work: the product became creator markets in the Farcaster / Base App feed. Continued in [Creator markets in the feed](../../creator-feed-markets/map.md).
