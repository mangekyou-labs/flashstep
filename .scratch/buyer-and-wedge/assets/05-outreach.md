## Drafts

Fill in `[Name]` and `[link]` before sending. The drafts are customer-discovery questions, not a sales pitch. Nothing is built yet, so none of them claim a product exists.

### 1. Coefficient Giving

**Subject:** Would you pay for a market-produced forecast on an AI question?

> Hi,
>
> I'm exploring a service for funders who already buy forecasts. You name a question, for example "Will a model score ≥X on [benchmark] by [date]?", and you get a probability produced by a funded prediction market instead of a forecaster panel. You set the budget ceiling, the market runs to a stated depth, and the question resolves through a named resolver.
>
> Coefficient Giving has commissioned forecasts before (Metaculus Pro Forecasters, FRI panels), so I'd value your honest reaction:
>
> 1. Would you pay for a forecast produced this way on an AI capability or release-timing question?
> 2. If so, roughly how much per question, or per series?
> 3. What would it have to beat what you buy today on (price, speed, accuracy, insider resistance)?
>
> A two-line reply is plenty, and so is "no".
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

### 2. Forecasting Research Institute

**Subject:** Market-based forecasts on AI questions: useful to you, or a competitor?

> Hi,
>
> I'm exploring a service that produces forecasts on funders' AI questions (capability thresholds, release timing) using a funded prediction market instead of an expert panel. FRI runs the leading AI expert panels, so your view would help me most:
>
> 1. Would FRI ever pay for, or co-run, a market-produced forecast alongside a panel, for example as a comparison arm in a study?
> 2. If so, roughly what budget per question or per study?
> 3. If not, why not? Accuracy, manipulation, regulation, or something else?
>
> A short reply, or a pointer to the right person, would be very welcome.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]
