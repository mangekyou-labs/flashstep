## Question

Which 30 crypto projects meet the bar in [The outreach plan](32-the-outreach-plan.md), and how is each one reached? The bar is at least one Base pool with ≥ US$100k liquidity and ≥ US$50k daily volume; a launchpad needs at least 5 tokens that meet it. Target about 18 token teams and 12 Base launchpads or communities, with Vietnamese and Asian projects first. For each, record: project, type, qualifying pool (address, liquidity, 24h volume, date checked), founder or BD lead, Telegram and X handles, partnership channel, and language (Vietnamese or English). Also list 10 fallback streamers and trading-group admins. Use primary sources (DEX analytics such as DEX Screener or GeckoTerminal, project sites, official socials).

Researched on 2026-10-26; full findings in [research/34-the-30-qualified-payers.md](../research/34-the-30-qualified-payers.md). The target of 30 was not reached, and the user chose to close with the shortfall.

None is Vietnamese-headquartered; AXIS Robotics (Singapore entity) and KOTAEMON (possible Japan link) are the only weak Asian hints. None has a named BD lead. Only 9 of 25 have a public Telegram, so the rest start at an X DM. Several sit near the US$50k volume floor: re-check before sending.
- **Launchpads and communities:** 0 confirmed at 5+ qualifying tokens. Virtuals is closest with 4. Clanker's count is inconclusive (spam-dominated top 100); Pangea and Doppler were not counted. The 5-token bar may be unattainable on Base today; lowering it is left to the user in [Run the outreach](../../run-the-outreach/task.md).
- **Fallback list:** 6 verified Vietnamese Telegram channels with subscriber counts (4 short of 10); named contacts for ThuanCapital, HC Capital, Crypto Family and AnhReview. No Vietnamese streamers found. Trading groups are promoters rather than pool-holding payers, so they look more like a distribution route.
